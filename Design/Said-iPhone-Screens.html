Rules carried through
Amber marks whoever is speaking, everywhere. Cards are stickers with a hard 3px edge, never a soft shadow. Timestamps, labels and counts are JetBrains Mono; everything else is Archivo. The blob is the record button, the avatar, the bullet, the scrubber thumb.
Before this gets built

Three things the design promises that iOS won’t give us

  • “A call — both sides.” iOS gives no third-party app access to phone-call audio. Apple’s own Call Recording is Phone-app only, with no public API. The mic hears your side plus whatever leaks from the earpiece — speakerphone works, held-to-ear does not. The source has to be renamed and re-described, or dropped.
  • “Learns each voice and names it as it goes.” Diarization today is a batch pass after the recording stops, and names are typed in by hand. Naming a voice live needs speaker enrollment, which Stage 1 explicitly deferred. Screens show Speaker 1 until someone renames them, or we scope enrollment in.
  • “My screen.” ReplayKit can record the iPhone’s own screen through a broadcast extension, so this is buildable — but it’s a new subsystem with its own process, not a port of the Mac’s ScreenCaptureKit path. Slide capture through the camera is the cheaper way to keep OCR on the timeline.

03 · Start

Three characters, not three rows

Your sheet, with the sources swapped for ones an iPhone can honestly deliver. Each keeps its brand colour and pressed edge so the choice still feels like picking a personality.

  • “A call” is gone. There’s no legitimate way to capture both sides. Pretending otherwise is a one-star review.
  • The toggle is a statement, not a setting — it’s on, it can’t be turned off, and it’s there to be read.
  • If screen recording gets scoped in, it becomes a fourth ink-coloured option here.

09 · Settings

Say what a switch costs

Grouped stickers instead of the Mac’s tabs. Every toggle with a real consequence — a download, a second stored copy, a battery hit — says so in the line underneath rather than in a help sheet.

  • Model choice is framed as accuracy, because nobody outside the repo knows what base.en means.
  • Missing next to the Mac: hotkeys, capture target, calendar auto-start.
  • Storage gets its own group. On a phone that number decides whether the app stays installed.

10 · Send

A session is a thing you can hand over

Until there’s an account, moving a session is a transfer rather than a sync — so make the object obvious. A .said bundle is the whole session folder zipped: words, audio, slides, everything.

  • The Mac claims the file type and imports it whole, keeping the session’s identity.
  • Size up front. A slide-heavy lecture is not a casual AirDrop.
  • One-way for now. Editing the same session on both devices is what needs the account, and that’s the later problem.
